### Create and Manage Your Team

A “team” in Microsoft Teams acts as a centralized workspace for your class, department, or project group. Setting it up correctly from the outset ensures efficient collaboration over time.

**To initiate a new team** (generally done by administrators):

* Provide the proposed team name
* Add a brief overview or description
* Assign an owner or team admin

Once the team is created:

**Add Members:**

1. Open the team, then click on "More options".
2. Choose "Manage Team".
3. Select “Add Member”, find users by name or email, then hit “Done”.

You can assign roles such as **Owner**, **Member**, or **Guest** to regulate access and permissions accordingly.

## Organize Content with Channels and Tabs

Microsoft Teams organizes group interaction and resources through **Channels**, while **Tabs** allow easy access to commonly used tools and materials.

**Channels:**

Every team comes with a default “General” channel. Additional channels can be added to focus discussions on particular subjects, themes, or group projects.

To create a new channel:

1. Click “More options” next to your team name.
2. Select “Add channel”.
3. Specify the name, set its privacy (Standard or Private), and include a short description.
4. Click “Add”.

**Tabs:**

Tabs appear at the top of every channel and provide quick links to important features:

* **Posts** – Structured conversation threads
* **Files** – Shared documents and folders
* **Class Notebook** – A collaborative OneNote workspace
* **Custom Tabs** – Integrate tools such as Forms, Planner, or third-party apps

This design helps reduce clutter and keeps key resources at your fingertips.

## Communicate Effectively with Students

Effective communication is a cornerstone of successful remote education. Microsoft Teams offers several tools to help maintain connection and engagement.

**Conversations and Posts:**

* Post messages in channels for everyone in the class to see
* Tag users or groups using “@mentions”
* Use reactions like emojis, likes, or GIFs to promote interaction

**Private Chats:**

* Initiate one-on-one or group conversations
* Useful for holding virtual office hours or private discussions
* Exchange messages, share files, or launch calls directly within the chat interface

**Engagement Tools:**

Make your digital presence more personable and interactive:

* Respond instantly with **Emojis**
* Use **GIFs** and **custom memes** via the Stickers feature
* Share documents and links that stay organized and searchable

## Schedule and Host Virtual Meetings

Microsoft Teams makes it simple to organize and conduct virtual meetings, lessons, or group discussions.

**Options to schedule a meeting:**

* Open the **Calendar** tab and select **+ New Meeting**
* Within a **channel**, click **Schedule a meeting** below the message field
* From a **chat**, use the calendar icon to arrange a meeting with others

Fill in the meeting details:

* Title of the meeting
* Start and end time
* Add a channel or invite attendees by email
* Choose recurrence (daily, weekly, etc.)

**To include external participants**, just type in their email address—they’ll get a join link via email.

**Before attending a meeting:**

* Test your video and audio settings
* Enable or disable your mic/camera as needed
* Use “Meeting Options” to assign roles, control lobby access, and more

**To join a meeting:**

* From your calendar: Click “Join” shortly before the meeting starts
* From a channel or chat: Hit “Join” on the meeting card
* From your inbox: Click the link provided in the invitation

Guests can join using a web browser, though signed-in users have access to the full feature set.

## Collaborate on Files and Assignments

Microsoft Teams is more than just a communication tool—it’s a robust platform for collaborative work and academic engagement.

**Sharing Files:**

* Upload files from your PC, OneDrive, or another channel
* All uploaded files are available in the **Files** tab for quick retrieval
* Collaborate on Office files like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint directly in Teams

**Collaboration Tools:**

* Use the **OneNote Class Notebook** for group brainstorming, journaling, or note sharing
* Add **Planner** tabs for managing tasks or **Forms** for surveys and quizzes

**Real-time editing** is supported, allowing students to work together simultaneously—ideal for team-based projects.

## Track Student Progress and Use Class Notebook

**Assignments and Grades:**

Microsoft Teams features a built-in system for managing coursework, tailored to educators:

* Design and distribute assignments
* Attach rubrics and tagging categories
* Provide feedback and oversee student submissions
* Grades are compiled in the **Grades tab**

Assignments can be pre-scheduled, duplicated for reuse, and sorted by student for tailored insights.

**Class Notebook:**

Each team has access to a built-in **Class Notebook**, which contains:

* **Collaboration Space** – For shared group activities
* **Content Library** – A read-only repository for key materials
* **Private Notebooks** – Individual sections for each student

The notebook supports handwriting input, audio recordings, equations, and embedded content—making it a powerful asset for digital education.
